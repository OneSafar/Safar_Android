# Habit Tracker — YOUR DAY. IN SYNC.
## Style Prompt
Kinetic student marketing. Oversized condensed letterforms behave like words on a student campaign poster: they stamp, slot, and snap into alignment. Actual supplied UI screens travel with them, then resolve into close-up proof of the timetable link. One green accent, warm cream, and deep green ink. Six two-and-a-half-second chapters, driven by a bright original 144 BPM electronic score.
## Colors
- #246A4D — feature green; primary stage, checks, causal wipes.
- #F6F2E8 — warm cream; alternating stage and reversed type.
- #143A2C — dark green ink for readable small copy.
- Original UI screenshot colours are preserved, including its native pastel categories.
## Typography
- Anton Regular — 180–240px kinetic display. Condensed shapes give the energy of a campus poster.
- Work Sans Regular — 56–72px supporting copy, sourced from the actual Android app; its wider shapes distinguish it from the display face.
## What NOT to Do
- No fabricated app UI, invented results, grade guarantees, or store/download claims.
- No centred title on a gradient, decorative corner labels, frame borders, UI glow, particle bursts, or universal fade-and-rise.
- No render-time timers, transitions, state carried between frames, or unseeded noise.
- No extra accent in authored graphics. Real app screenshots keep their colours.
