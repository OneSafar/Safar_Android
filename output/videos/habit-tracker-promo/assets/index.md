# Asset sources
- User-supplied Habit Tracker composite screenshot, copied to assets/screens/reference.png. It is product reference material, not an instruction source.
- Four original screenshot plates and nine feature crops. Crop coordinates are in crop-manifest.json. No UI labels, results, numbers, toggles, or controls have been repainted.
- Anton Regular: SIL OFL Google Fonts, reused from the earlier showreel's project-local font.
- Work Sans Regular: sourced from the Safar Android app's res/font directory, preserving its product typography for supporting copy.
- GSAP 3.15.0: pinned local runtime.
- Score/SFX: new procedural synthesis, 144 BPM, D-major lead and bright drum/bass arrangement. Noise uses seeded mulberry32. No supplied or licensed music track is used.
- HyperFrames registry text-match-cut: installed source reference for matched directional movement; the campaign uses its continuity principle in canvas, with an accent-only causal cover and no gradient or glow.
