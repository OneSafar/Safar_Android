Concept 1: Quick Focus & Pomodoro Launcher

Grid: 2x2 or 3x2

Purpose: Zero friction to start studying. The student sees one-tap presets for 25m Pomodoro, 50m Deep Work, or free Stopwatch, plus ambient study sounds, without opening the app first.

Deep Links: safar://ekagra?mode=25
Target Users: Pomodoro & Deep study enthusiasts

Key Highlights & Interactions

  • ✓ Instant Timer Trigger: Tapping "25m" or "50m" fires a PendingIntent directly launching `TimerService` without waiting for the full UI cold launch.
  • ✓ Streak Preservation: Displays the current streak count as a daily reminder not to break the habit chain.
  • ✓ Ambient Sound Fast-Toggle: Allows switching between White Noise, Rain, or Silent Study before the session starts.
  • ✓ Size Efficiency: Fits comfortably in standard 2x2 or 3x2 launcher slots on Samsung OneUI, Pixel Launcher, and Xiaomi HyperOS.
