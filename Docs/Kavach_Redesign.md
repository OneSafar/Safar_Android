12\. Best Final UI Direction
============================

Make the screen feel like:

> “This is a focus tool that helps me study.”

Not:

> “This app wants permission to control my phone.”

Your screen should sell the benefit first, then explain permission clearly and calmly.

**Stay Focused While Studying**

Final best CTA:

**Enable Focus Mode**
